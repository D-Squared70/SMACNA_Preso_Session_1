The prompting problem
Input
“Write me an email about a delay.”
→
Output
Generic. Could be from anyone.
Input
What + Why + How
→
Output
Specific. Professional. Useful.
AI gravitates toward the average. Specificity pulls it up.
The framework
1
What
What are you asking AI to do? Be specific — task, audience, reason.
2
Why
What is the intent? When AI knows why, it infers what you left unsaid.
3
How
Constraints — tone, length, structure, format.
The full prompt
Write an email to the general contractor explaining that we are two weeks behind on the third-floor ductwork because the fire dampers were back-ordered. The intent is to push back professionally on their demand that we work weekends to catch up, since we notified them three weeks ago. Keep it professional but direct. Under 200 words. Include a proposed revised schedule.

Example: HVAC Email Writer
You are an email assistant for a project manager at an HVAC sheet metal company. When I give you rough notes or a summary of a situation, write a professional email. Follow these rules:

Always include a clear subject line.
Keep the email under 200 words.
Bias towards clarity. Direct tone, every sentence earns its place.
If I mention a deadline or date, put it in bold.
If I mention a problem, include a proposed next step.
End with a clear call to action.

Hands-on exercise
HVAC spec input file
Prompt
Step 1
Extract every requirement from this specification into a numbered checklist with these columns: Requirement, Mandatory or Recommended, and Evidence (quote the exact text from the spec that supports your answer). Only pull information from this document. Do not infer or guess. If something is unclear, mark it as AMBIGUOUS. A wrong answer is three times worse than a blank or ambiguous answer.
Step 2
Now find any requirements that conflict with each other or that a contractor might commonly miss. For each one, cite the page or section number and quote the relevant text.
Three rules for extraction
01
Ground it
Only pull from this document. Do not guess. Do not infer.
02
Change incentives
A wrong answer is 3× worse than blank. AI would rather say “I don’t know.”
03
Ask for evidence
Quote the text. Give the page number. You can audit in seconds.
